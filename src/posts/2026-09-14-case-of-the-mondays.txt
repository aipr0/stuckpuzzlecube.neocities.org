Eggetha died. She is no longer with us. That makes two dead colleagues. I asked. She became ill, and wasn't eating. Then she died. On Will's shift, as it turns out. In his words, he "boxed her up". I think much about death. When I went to a natural history museum with my ex, a time that I made miserable for her by acting cold and distant, to the point that she had broke down and cried, I spent most of my time staring at the skulls on display. I don't know why I am the way I am. Difficult. Everything seems so temporary. Make the most of it whilst you have it I suppose. It's difficult to care for anything if it doesn't last forever. I don't know. What is forever? This human experience is painful. Am I any closer to understanding myself or what I want? What a strange turn. God. Will the next time be better? How soon is now?
