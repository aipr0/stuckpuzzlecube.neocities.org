I slept most all day. I got up. I ate. Went back to sleep. I dreamt I was in this beautiful, sprawling, high-ceiling mall. We were all getting fitted out to play airsoft. I had a sniper rifle. I was the last one to the arena. It was dark. It was a lot of fun. Because of my right-eye amblyopia I had to resort to quickscoping people with the laser sight to aim. There was someone else who was topping the kill board and we never saw each other.

My cousin Jade picked me up and we went to this cottage inn that my Nan likes to have functions at. We had a meal, it was my Granddad's 70th birthday party. His birthday is tomorrow. I lugged his new air fryer in, bow on top. It was nice seeing family. I brought my Steam Deck along and drew more attention than I expected or wanted. Everyone now is on their phones, including the older folk. Well my aunt who is the most strict out of the lot took a strange interest in it. She was amazed. Odd. I also told her about my medication and she took an interest in that; she has been on amitriptyline in the past. I had hunter's chicken, and an oreo cheesecake for dessert. My Granddad had a drum kit themed birthday cake, it was a fruit cake. I took some home.

I left to get some cigarettes after Jade had dropped me off home, and this woman on her bike struck up a conversation with me after noticing the colourful bag of cake I had. I walked along with her for a while and we talked. She said she had just come back from some dance party. She gave me her number and said she would let me know about the next one she finds out about. I'm looking forward to it.
