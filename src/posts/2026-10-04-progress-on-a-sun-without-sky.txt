I'm happy to announce I have made some progress with the story that I most want to write, 'A Sun Without Sky'. I have come up with a very satisfactory first act to the story. I have developed more of the middle arc, the protagonist, and a little more of the world. I also think I better understand the feel of it. I originally wanted to convey true horror to the reader, but have no skill or experience with that style of writing. I think it's difficult to really scare someone with a written story. Then I realised, it isn't a slasher horror, or something like that. It's a psychological horror. It is the horror that I myself experienced. And I can't make you feel what I've felt. But I can explain it to you, I can write about it in a way you can imagine. I know the last chapter now too. What I need now is to give it the globetrotting adventure, the intrigue, how I feel on the philosophy of how technology has shaped human culture and society, and well, just generally to make it interesting. Whilst it is primarily a science fiction horror, it has always been my intention to make an interesting and well-thought out story set in a mostly plausible or explainable cyberpunk fantasy world.

Always your friend,
- Riddler (T.S)
