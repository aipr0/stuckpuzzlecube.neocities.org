Nothing but gloom. I check my feed. All vague suggestions that the worst possible thing is happening right now. This kind of negativity, it's a black virulent poison. It feeds and it spreads and self-perpetuates. I myself have been a pessimist and a cynic for too long. Somewhere along the way I swapped sides and joined the losing team. See! Even now, that mark of loathing on me shows still. The dark in my heart, the tar on my soul. It is easy to dismiss the hopeful youth as naive, and there is some truth to it. But on the other face of that truth is the fact that it takes heart to have hope. A thought that has fallen out of my head for some time only now to resurface, is that if someone such as me can come around to show such optimism, then surely it is possible for others, in fact, not only must it be possible, but whatever has influenced me to change, those influences must already be working on others too. But it is hard, not only to be positive, but also to show it. How easy it is to snidely shoot down others with short and thoughtless remarks. It is easier to give in. This is your lot, and it's easy to think that. Because it feels bad, it must be truthful. This is the ugly truth about pessimism. It is easy.

That being said, I am functionally a pessimist. There are few things that I feel are worth leaving to chance, and those few are typically far outside of my sphere of influence. The fate of the species for example. How can you best prepare for nuclear armageddon, global martial law, or the collapse of civilisation? You can't to any reasonable degree. You can only wish for the best. However, I am now presently concerned with the fate of the internet. Perhaps we've had it good for too long. It was never really meant to be this way. As much as it enables the rich and the powerful, it enables everyone else too. It is maybe as even a playing field as there is currently. We are all of us first anonymous before anything else after coming online. Everything that follows is a result of our actions. And one person can wield immense power online, for good or not or worse.

But now I will drive towards what I really mean to say. Now more than ever am I working towards the personal archival of as much as is feasible from the web. There may come a day where infrastructure is attacked and becomes crippled. A day where the state imposes its will to fragment and control the web absolutely, all said and done for the sake of the children no doubt. This boils my blood. Children, a convenient excuse for control, an unfortunate statistic for anything else. I musn't get too distracted. The archival of as much as is feasible from the web. It would not fit any other purpose but to be for my own personal collection. And what I want is well, trivial. Entertainment. Educational literature. I dread to think. Have we taken this all for granted? Could we someday lose this freedom? The mind reels.

I have no plague or war or the end of times to sell to you. And I am no student of history, no expert in technology, nor philosopher of man, so I have no convincing basis of which should concern you. I only understand that those who are rich and powerful are exponentially less concerned when it comes to the problem of the masses. If the 99% lose all purpose in the eyes of the 1%, then why would they imagine a future designed to accompany our wants or needs at all? We all may well be walking hand-in-hand into the ghetto. At least in the end, I will still be able to watch My Little Pony.

Let's hope that such an awful future for the species shall never occur!
