One thing I'll always tell people when they're down is to try and be positive. This never lands well. And it's frustrating for the both of us, but it is the most real and honest advice that you can give. We have this idea that there really is the right combination of words spoken like a secret incantation that can lift all dark clouds and move mountains. The inspirational rally cry, or a legendary motivational speech. The real answer most of the time is a lot more boring and uninteresting. Want to be happy? Don't do drugs, surround yourself with happy people. Want to be rich? Don't buy into a get-rich-quick scheme, get an education and work hard.

Why do I bring this up? Well I have ups and downs. The thing about being down is that it is easy to stay like that. Depression. A flat line. The dull lull. Why is it that when we're riding on high we have to come down? Because you get bored of being happy. And yes, people LOVE to be unhappy. So you come down and you're unhappy for a while. For some people they have the "high highs" and the "low lows", and for some people they're never really happy or unhappy. The same can be said about being unhappy; you can get bored of that too. It's the seeds of optimism that help sprout you up and out of it. You have to maintain a positive outlook even when you're upset. To frame everything as positively as possible. There is always two sides to everything. If you can appreciate that, and respect the power that comes from looking on the bright side, then you'll be happy.

Happiness is a choice, even if it's a hard one to make.
