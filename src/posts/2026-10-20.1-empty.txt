My manager asked me to cover a shift tomorrow. Something in the vein of "I really don't want to have to ask but are you okay to cover a shift tomorrow? Only if you're well though". I accepted just to tug on any loose threads holding my job together. Taking sick in the middle of a night shift does not look good and is not something I can afford to repeat. I need help, soon.

I already spoke about writing a reply to my ex. The post office was shut today. I will send it tomorrow. I'm still due to write a few other letters to friends I am actually in more regular contact with.

I spent the evening playing Fallout 3. I had a craving. I'd say it feels more empty than I remember, but it's really just that I know it inside and out by now.

Here I am half-assedly worrying about my immediate mortality, when the fate of the species has never seemed less certain. Things grow longer, darker, and more dire. I must remain optimistic but for what? It's unlikely that I will raise a family, unless I decide to make that my life's purpose. That is its own set of sets of problems. For love? How incompatible I am, and so much can be said for most others. I have not given up entirely on the idea of love but I certainly have a much more jaded, and "temporary" idea of what love realistically can be. So. For what else? Great personal accomplishment? It is too late in life to be a genius in anything. I can aspire to be great, with excruciating effort. Then for what? Wishful thinking for everlasting peace and quiet, or unbridled hedonism? Ultimately, no matter your disposition, may you have such lust for life, or may you be a sloth, who if anyone can get excited for that? Not for a promise mind you, but a hope. Hope that someday we may all be immortal and free to be present with all five senses in waking dreams. I can only see the more immediate future. Will the masses rebel just in time? Or will the species end with the last of the technocrats fighting each other for more land to fill their barren, irradiated demesnes? I wish now more than ever for God and Heaven. But I cannot pray as I am without faith. And I am terrified of dying. As I am also of thinking too much of living forever. Becoming bored. Or knowing that as infinite as a lifetime can seem with all manner of tricks in the virtue of time dilation, some day everything must end.

How close we are born in the current era, how close we are to the potential of becoming functionally immortal. To be born another hundred years from now would certainly guarantee it. But for us, now? We must pray that the world does not become any less stable than it already is. We will be cutting it close, and that is only if everything else remains smooth.
