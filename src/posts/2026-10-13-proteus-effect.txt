Something I came across that I found interesting: the "Proteus Effect".

Taken from Wikipedia:
"The Proteus effect describes a phenomenon in which the behavior of an individual, within virtual worlds, is changed by the characteristics of their avatar."

This is something I want to read more about. Apparently according to some studies from nearly twenty years ago, someone's online avatar has an effect on the way they act. Assigned a comic book superhero avatar? Apparently you'll act nicer, and vice versa. But also, apparently you'll act according to how you perceive others will expect someone with your avatar to act.

I wonder about this, about me personally. Have I might have acted differently because of the avatars that I've used. I can neither confirm or deny it. I would say yes and no. The studies I've glanced over have been conducted a long time ago, during a different culture of internet. A different culture and time completely. I have always picked a profile picture to reflect something about myself, my interests, or my emotions, or interest in a character. It seems like a chicken and egg sort of thing. Have I acted differently BECAUSE of what I've chosen as an avatar, or is that just a reflection of how I have already chosen to act? Let's face it. I am an eccentric person. I don't like to use that word "eccentric" because to me, and maybe opposed to quite a few others, I find eccentricity endearing. I like different, I like weird. So it seems a bit egotistical to use that word to describe myself. But to call myself weird or abnormal, well, that's just plain negative. So anyway, I don't know if I'm the normal candidate for someone to be influenced by this proposed Proteus effect. It is interesting though. And I am very curious how this extends to virtual reality avatars.

I really need to see if there are more recent studies exploring this idea. I am young and pessimistic, and I am living in such strange times, as we all are.
