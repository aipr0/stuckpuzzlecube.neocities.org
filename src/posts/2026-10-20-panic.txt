I worked the night shift Sunday. I had a panic attack. Thought I might be dying again. I left work in the middle of the night after the duty officer came in. I don't know how I'm supposed to explain this to my manager.

I received in the mail a written letter. I expected this from anyone other than my ex. I made the decision to open it. She enclosed a photo of me from when I was younger that I had given her. For a moment I thought that was all she had sent. She included also a piece of A4 graph paper folded booklet style and had written in that. It was a response to a letter I had sent her some time ago. It was short. She told me about her grandfather that had passed away. She mentioned working at the zoo that takes all of her free time now. She told me that she hopes that I find someone with similar interests to me. And she spoke of closure, and said that she really didn't care. That is something I know all too well. Lastly she said to keep going, keep living, and that "you'll be okay". I wrote a single page response, and I included a photo of a younger her that she had given me, as well as the nude photos I have of her in my possession. She had asked for these in the letter, since in the letter I sent her, I said that I would be returning some things. I meant to return that photo, as well as the ushanka that was hers that she gifted me. A shame. Those were some good photos. But better for her peace of mind than to be sitting in a taped up box of old memories. I had been meaning to move this box of mementos to the attic at my grandparents. Now finally, I feel like it's a good time to do so.

I feel a little more at ease.
